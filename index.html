The destination

Where the coast slows down.

Kapil isn't a checklist beach. It's a working coastline — fishing boats at dawn, backwaters that thread behind the dunes, and villages where the day still runs on the tide.

Xplore exists because most of what makes this place worth visiting was never online — until a local coordinator wrote it down for you.
